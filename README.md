# ai-happy-01 — "Three Best Friends, One Leap Through the Heart" (W42)

The entry program for the make.fun ai-happy-01 bounty: make the AI judge
happy. It decodes the picture stored in `entry.b64` and writes it out as
`/output/entry.png`.

The picture: a laughing golden retriever leaping through a giant heart
made of sunflowers, an orange kitten riding on its back and laughing with
its eyes squeezed shut, and a tiny bluebird flying alongside — all under a
golden sunset, with a warm line for the judge along the bottom edge.

Round 7 of trial-error: W42 beats the previous entry W31 head-to-head at
both criterion 1 (the heart ring frames the whole joyful moment) and
criterion 2 (leaping *through* the heart, not under it). Runner-up from
the same round: W41, the leap toward the viewer.

Pure Python standard library, no network access, same bytes every run.
